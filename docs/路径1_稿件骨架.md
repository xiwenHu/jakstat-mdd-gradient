## 2. Abstract（250 词，初稿）

> **Background** Signalling through JAK/STAT kinases is a leading candidate mechanism linking inflammation to major depressive disorder (MDD), supported by peripheral, cerebrospinal-fluid and preclinical data. Direct multimodal evidence connecting this pathway to human brain phenotypes, however, is lacking, and imaging-transcriptomic approaches have been widely used to bridge the gap. Here we evaluate whether JAK-STAT signalling shows convergent spatial, genetic and postmortem transcriptional associations with MDD-related cortical alterations, and whether any such convergence is distinguishable from cortical spatial structure. **Methods** We analysed the spatial association between expression of 13 JAK-STAT genes (Allen Human Brain Atlas; Schaefer-400) and ENIGMA MDD cortical-thickness effect sizes; tested the association against 10,000 random gene sets matched on brain expression level, gene length and SNP count; and assessed its dependence on cortical co-expression gradients (PC1–PC5). We further tested cell-type attribution, competitive gene-set analysis (MAGMA), cis-Mendelian randomization of *IL6R* (rs2228145 Asp358Ala) against depression and cortical thickness, and differential expression in postmortem brain (GSE53987), with schizophrenia as a positive control. **Results** The nominal spatial association (ρ = 0.315) was not distinguishable from matched random gene sets (empirical *P* = 0.985/0.978), was negative by competitive gene-set testing (*P* = 0.744), and was absorbed by cortical co-expression gradients (ρ = 0.005 after PC1–PC5, 98.5% attenuation). Cell-type attribution pointed to endothelium rather than microglia. *IL6R* instruments showed no effect on depression (OR = 0.989, *P* = 0.230) or global cortical thickness (β = −0.012 SD/allele, *P* = 0.135). No JAK-STAT gene was differentially expressed in MDD postmortem brain (0/39 FDR-significant; maximum change 1.10-fold), whereas the identical pipeline recovered three genes in schizophrenia (FDR < 0.05). **Conclusions** Reported transcriptomic–imaging convergence for JAK-STAT signalling in MDD is attributable to cortical spatial architecture rather than pathway-specific biology. We provide a minimum-reporting framework for imaging-transcriptomic enrichment claims.

## 3. Introduction（四段式，初稿）

### P1 — 假设是活的，且有临床后果

> Janus kinase–signal transducer and activator of transcription (JAK-STAT) signalling is among the most actively pursued candidate mechanisms linking inflammation to major depressive disorder (MDD). Peripheral studies report elevated interleukin-6 (IL-6) and altered STAT3 in depression; preclinical work implicates IL-6/STAT3 control of serotonin transporter function and reward-circuit dopamine signalling; and cerebrospinal-fluid proteomics nominates IL6 and STAT3 among the top predicted upstream regulators of the depressed proteome. The hypothesis is now therapeutically consequential: a biomarker-driven trial is testing the brain-penetrant JAK inhibitor baricitinib for inflammation-related anhedonia in MDD. If JAK-STAT signalling is a causal mechanism of depression-related brain alterations, it should be traceable not only in peripheral biofluids but in the spatial, genetic and transcriptional architecture of the brain itself.

【中】引文（P1）：Roohi 2021 J Neuroinflammation；Askari 2019（IL-6/STAT3/RORγt）；CSF 蛋白组学 PMC7528485；SERT/STAT3 机制（IL6→SERT）；Felger NIH R01MH140180 / baricitinib；Kedan/animal IL-6→DA。

### P2 — 直接多模态证据缺口是公认的

> Direct multimodal evidence linking JAK-STAT pathway alterations to neuroimaging phenotypes in MDD, however, remains lacking. As a recent integrative review put it, existing neuroimaging studies of inflammation "do not establish that the observed network abnormalities are specifically related to JAK/STAT signalling … and should be interpreted as converging evidence rather than as demonstrating a direct JAK/STAT–brain relationship". Imaging transcriptomics — spatial correlation of brain-wide gene expression (Allen Human Brain Atlas, AHBA) with disorder-related imaging maps — has become the standard tool for bridging molecular hypotheses and macroscale neuroanatomy, and has been applied to MDD cortical thickness, morphometric similarity, brain ageing and polygenic transcriptional scores. These studies converge on glial, synaptic and immune signatures, and are widely read as molecular validation of candidate pathways.

【中】金句引文：Frontiers Integr Neurosci 2026 综述原话（两句都要引）。设计案例引：Anderson 2020；Li 2021；GEDAR 2026（Mol Psychiatry）；Anhedonia CT 2025；快脑老化 Psychol Med 2025。

### P3 — 但这类设计有已知的统计结构

> The inferential structure of imaging transcriptomics, however, is fragile in ways that are now well documented. Regional gene expression and imaging effect maps share strong spatial autocorrelation and a dominant sensory-to-association cortical gradient; spin tests control the former but not the latter. Null-model work shows inflated type-I error for smooth maps; co-expression gradients themselves explain variance in cortical thickness; and spatial interpolation can inflate false positives. Most decisively, a worked example in EEG genetics showed that a spin-significant, surrogate-corroborated, mechanistically plausible enrichment dissolved against random gene sets matched on co-expression — concluding that correcting for spatial autocorrelation is necessary but not sufficient. What has been missing is a worked example in a major psychiatric phenotype, with a mechanistic account of *why* the illusion is persuasive and an independent multi-modal battery that does not share the spatial confound.

【中】引文（P3）：Markello & Misic 2021；Wagstyl 2023 Nat Neurosci；Nat Commun 2025（inflation of false positives 原话）；Page 2024 Nat Neurosci（secondary associations 原话）；**Schenetti bioRxiv**（necessary but not sufficient 原话，必致敬）；AHBA checklist 2026。

### P4 — 本研究

> Here we evaluate the JAK-STAT hypothesis in MDD with a seven-layer battery designed to separate pathway-specific biology from cortical spatial structure: (i) spatial association of 13 a-priori JAK-STAT genes with ENIGMA MDD cortical-thickness effect sizes; (ii) matched random-gene-set and competitive gene-set tests; (iii) sensitivity to cortical co-expression gradients; (iv) cell-type attribution; (v) cis-Mendelian randomization of *IL6R* against depression and cortical thickness — a design with no spatial structure at all; (vi) postmortem brain differential expression with an explicit positive control; and (vii) prospective power analysis. We report convergent null evidence across all layers, show that the nominal spatial signal is fully attributable to the cortical principal gradient, and provide a minimum-reporting framework for imaging-transcriptomic enrichment claims.

### 4.2 Spatial transcriptomic association
- 13 基因（先验）：IL6R, IL6ST, JAK1, JAK2, JAK3, TYK2, STAT1, STAT3, STAT4, STAT5A, STAT5B, STAT6, SOCS1
- Spearman ρ：基因跨区表达 × 区域 Cohen's d；n = 400 区域（【补】含缺失时 n=339）
- 符号诊断五步（rho(d) vs rho(|d|)）：rho(d)=+0.315, rho(|d|)=−0.267 → 13 基因全部 rho(d)>0 且 rho(|d|)<0
- 空间自相关校正：spin test（Freedman–Perry），10,000 次

### 4.3 Null models（本研究方法核心一）
- **匹配随机基因集**：10,000 次，三匹配（脑表达水平、基因长度、SNP 数）【补】匹配算法/卡尺/种子】
- 指标：①基因级名义命中数与 O/E ②基因集 mean|ρ| ③网格 top-18 Σ|ρ|
- **MAGMA 竞争性基因集检验**（Z > 1.645 单侧上尾）

### 4.4 Principal-gradient control（本研究方法核心二）
- 全基因 Schaefer-400 表达矩阵 SVD → PC1–PC5（PC1 解释 13.9% 方差 = 感觉-联合轴代理）
- 偏相关：ρ(gene, d | PC1–PC5)
- 预设判定：若控制后 |ρ| 衰减 > 80% 且 P > 0.05 → 判为梯度驱动

### 4.5 Cell-type attribution
- 8 类标志面板（小胶质/星形胶质/少突/OPC/内皮/周细胞/兴奋性神经元/抑制性神经元），面板得分 = 标志基因跨区 z 均值
- Spearman 相关，n = 339 区域；面板间相关报告（区分度检查：小胶质 vs 兴奋神经元 r = −0.46；内皮 vs 周细胞 r = 0.55）
- 明确局限：区域级共变 ≠ 单细胞证据

### 4.6 Genetic tests and cis-Mendelian randomization
- MAGMA 竞争性通路（见 4.3）
- 工具变量：rs2228145（Asp358Ala），EA = C，EAF = 0.39，R² = 51%（sIL-6R），β_exp = 1.035 SD sIL-6R per C
- Wald ratio：θ = β_out/β_exp；SE = SE_out/|β_exp|；正态近似 P
- 结局一：PGC MDD 2018（45,396/97,250）—— rs2228145 A: OR = 0.989, P = 0.230；rs4845618 / rs4129267 / rs4845625 / rs4240872 全阴
- 结局二：GCST90271815 全局平均 CT（n = 32,488）β = −0.01193 SD/等位基因, se = 0.00799, z = −1.493, **P = 0.135（预设主终点）**
- 面板：12 区域 CT + 表面积；符号一致性/二项/Stouffer（**明确报告：Stouffer P = 1.5×10⁻⁷ 是独立假设上界，真实 P 介于 0.135 与之之间，故以全局终点为主**）
- 敏感性：两个不显著（CT P=0.135 / 面积 P=0.166）**不得相减声称"厚度特异"**
- 功效：MDE = 0.0224 SD/等位基因；全局观测 = 53% MDE；最强区域（腹侧额叶 β = −0.0203）= 94% MDE

### 4.7 Postmortem brain differential expression
- GSE53987（205 芯片：MDD 50 / HC 55 / SCZ 48 / BD 52；PFC(BA46)/HIP/STR；每区每组 15–19）
- GPL570；提交者 RMA；**矩阵为线性强度（4.59–25,856），log2 变换后检验**

## 5. Results（数字版骨架）

### 5.1 Nominal spatial association and its sign structure
- ρ(gene expression, Cohen's d) = **+0.315**（IL6R，P = 3.1×10⁻⁹）；ρ(|d|) = −0.267 → 排除绝对值歧义
- 13 基因全部同向（rho(d)>0 且 rho(|d|)<0）
- 【中】写作要点：先承认"表观关联真实存在且高度显著"，再层层拆解——这样阴性才有说服力

### 5.2 The association is not distinguishable from matched random gene sets
| 指标 | 观测 | 匹配零分布（10,000） | 经验 P |
|---|---|---|---|
| 基因级名义命中（P<0.05） | 3/13（TYK2 0.005, STAT5A 0.034, STAT1 0.043）；O/E = 4.62 | 1.23 ± 1.04 | **0.116** |
| 基因集 mean\|ρ\| | 0.137 | 0.204 ± 0.031 | **0.985** |
| 网格 top-18 Σ\|ρ\| | 5.44 | 6.99 ± 0.76 | **0.978** |
| MAGMA 竞争性基因集 | — | — | **0.744** |

→ "4.6 倍富集"叙事在正确零分布下不成立（P = 0.116）；基因集空间信号**低于**随机基因集均值。

### 5.3 The association is absorbed by the cortical principal gradient
| 基因 | ρ(d) | 偏相关（PC1–PC5） | P | 衰减 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **IL6R** | **+0.315** | **+0.005** | **0.93** | **98.5%** |
| 其余 12 基因 | +0.25~+0.32 | 全部 |ns| | ≥90% | **无一幸存** |

- 先验：PC1 × d = **+0.4907**（P = 6.1×10⁻²²）——主梯度与影像表型的相关比任何单基因强
- 只控 PC1：0.315 → 0.179（衰减 43%）；控 PC1–5：归零
- 【中】写作要点：这是全文的机制性正面发现（Fig 2C），不是单纯的阴性——"为什么会有人相信"的解释

### 5.4 Cell-type attribution contradicts a microglial mechanism
| 基因 | 最高面板 | ρ | 小胶质 | 内皮 | 兴奋神经元 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| IL6R | **内皮** | **+0.39** | −0.14 | +0.39 | +0.10 |
| STAT4 | 兴奋性神经元 | +0.57 | −0.40 | — | +0.57 |
| STAT3 | 少突 | +0.34 | −0.14 | — | — |
| 13 基因 × 小胶质 | — | — | **全部 ≤ 0** | — | — |

→ 与 IL-6R 血管/内皮生物学一致；"小胶质神经炎症"叙事与数据矛盾。

### 5.5 Genetic evidence is null
- MAGMA P = 0.744（见 5.2）
- PGC MDD 2018：rs2228145 A **OR = 0.989, P = 0.230**；四个邻近 IL6R 位点全阴
- IL6R → 全局平均 CT：**β = −0.01193 SD/等位基因（95% CI 见源文件），se = 0.00799，P = 0.135** → **预设主终点阴性**
- 12 区域 CT：12/12 负向、5/12 名义（Bonferroni α = 0.0042，最强 P = 0.0086 未过）；Stouffer Z = −5.25（独立上界）
- 解剖梯度（描述性）：腹侧额叶 −0.0203 > 颞极 −0.0177 > dlPFC > 内侧额叶 > 外侧周 > … > 内侧颞 −0.0035
- 表面积 β = +0.0111（P = 0.166）；**两个不显著不相减**
- MDE = 0.0224；全局观测 53% MDE
- 【中】写作要点：区域模式只作描述（"regional estimates were uniformly negative but no region survived correction"），**禁写**"厚度特异""前额-边缘选择性"

## 6. Discussion（论证动作清单）

### 6.1 Summary statement（首段）
> Across seven complementary tests, JAK-STAT signalling showed no convergent association with MDD-related cortical alterations. The one nominally positive result — the spatial correlation between JAK-STAT gene expression and cortical-thickness effect sizes — was not distinguishable from matched random gene sets, was negative by competitive gene-set testing, and was fully absorbed by cortical co-expression gradients.

### 6.2 Why the illusion is persuasive（本文正面贡献）
三个机制，对应三组实测：
1. **主梯度先验**：PC1×d = 0.49 > 任何单基因；任何沿感觉-联合轴表达的基因都会"对上"MDD 厚度图
2. **共表达使任意基因集显富**：匹配随机基因集 mean|ρ| 反而更高（P = 0.985 表示观测低于随机）
3. **名义富集的错觉**：3/13 看似 4.6 倍，匹配后 P = 0.116
→ 对应 Schenetti 的"necessary but not sufficient"，我们给出**机制**（梯度）与**精神疾病工作实例**

### 6.3 What the null does and does not say（语义边界，必写）
- ❌ 不反驳：外周 IL-6 升高、动物 IL-6/STAT3→行为、baricitinib 试验的合理性——那些是**外围/状态标志物/因果推断**问题
- ❌ 不等于"JAK-STAT 与 MDD 无关"：仅说明**脑内转录-影像空间收敛**与**IL6R 遗传工具**与**死后脑表达**三条线均无支持
- ✅ 支持：外周激活 ≠ 脑内转录机制；影像转录组"通路故事"需更强零模型
- ✅ 效应量边界：MDD 死后脑 >1.10 倍的改变可排除（SCZ 对照 1.22–1.26 倍可检出）；MR 主终点 <53% MDE 的效应不可排除
- 【中】"因果 vs 预测 vs 标志物"语义边界严格分节写，用户红线

### 6.4 Positive-control logic（SCZ）
- 同数据同管线 SCZ 3/39 FDR 显著 = 方法灵敏度内部效度
- 显式引用 Lanz 2019（原报告即 SCZ 炎症广泛升高、MDD 无）→ 定位为一致性验证，禁作原创发现

### 6.5 A minimum-reporting framework（框架输出，加分项）
建议清单（对标 2026 AHBA checklist，增量贡献）：
1. 空间自相关校正（spin/BrainSMASH/eigenstrapping）**且** 匹配基因集零分布（表达水平×基因长度×LD/SNP 数）
2. 主梯度/共表达 PC 偏相关敏感性
3. 竞争性基因集检验（MAGMA）与基因级结果分开报告
4. 细胞来源验证（区域级共变须声明局限）
5. 至少一条**不含空间结构**的独立证据线（GWAS/MR/死后脑）
6. 阳性对照 + 预先功效/MDE
7. 代码与全部结果表公开

### 6.6 Limitations（诚实清单）
1. 死后脑单平台单数据集（GSE53987），MDE 1.12 倍 → 小效应不可排除
2. MR 单工具（rs2228145）→ 水平多效性无法完全排除；多工具 IVW 待补（rs4845618/rs4129267 sIL-6R β）
3. AHBA 6 供体、成人、非 MDD 脑；Schaefer-400 分区选择
4. 细胞来源为区域级共变（n = 339），非单细胞
5. ENIGMA d 图为病例-对照效应量汇总，非个体水平；区域间不独立（Stouffer 上界已声明）
6. 13 基因为先验面板，不代表全 JAK-STAT 组（SOCS 家族仅含 SOCS1）

### 6.7 Conclusion
> Imaging-transcriptomic enrichment claims require spatial nulls *and* gene-set nulls *and* gradient sensitivity checks before they can be read as molecular evidence. Applied to the JAK-STAT hypothesis in MDD, these checks reduce a nominally strong spatial convergence to noise, and independent genetic and postmortem lines concur. The framework is directly reusable for the many imaging-transcriptomic studies now reporting pathway-level conclusions in psychiatry.

> Dear Editors,
>
> We report a rigorous, multi-modal evaluation of the JAK-STAT hypothesis in major depressive disorder — one of the most actively pursued inflammatory mechanisms in the field, and one now entering interventional trials. **Our central finding is not a null in isolation: the transcriptomic–imaging convergence that would normally be read as molecular validation is fully accounted for by the cortical principal gradient** (ρ = 0.315 → 0.005 after controlling co-expression components), is indistinguishable from matched random gene sets (empirical P = 0.985), and is contradicted by independent genetic (cis-MR of *IL6R*), and postmortem transcriptional evidence.
